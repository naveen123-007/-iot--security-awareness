IoT Security Awareness

Internet of Things (IoT) devices are connected devices that can communicate through the internet.

📺 Smart TV

Smart TVs connect to the internet and may contain personal information.

📷 CCTV Camera

Poorly protected cameras can create privacy and security risks.

⌚ Smart Watch

Smart watches can collect personal and activity data.

🔊 Smart Speaker

Smart speakers are connected to the internet and may process voice information.
